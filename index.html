How it plays

Serve members

Tap a machine or a pad to put members to work. Every workout earns money, and your gym keeps earning while you're away.

Upgrade and expand

Level up your equipment, collect all 30 machines and open 10 rooms. Reach level 50 and you've built a legend.

Make it yours

Name your gym, drag to look around and pinch to zoom. Keep several save slots and tune the music and sound effects.
